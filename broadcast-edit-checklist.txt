KHS PRESS / LEARN — CANVA BROADCAST EDIT CHECKLIST

Before editing
[ ] One clear takeaway; interview and relevant B-roll ready.
[ ] Landscape 1920 x 1080 practice project; confirm producer requirements.

During editing
[ ] Trim dead air and mistakes without changing meaning.
[ ] Close gaps and preserve complete words.
[ ] Match B-roll to the interview; keep the voice continuous.
[ ] Mute competing B-roll sound; listen to every segment.
[ ] Verify lower-third name and role; use readable text and safe margins.

Before handoff
[ ] Watch the whole timeline for gaps, rough cuts, and clipped words.
[ ] Download the full video as MP4 (1080p practice target).
[ ] Watch the downloaded file from beginning to end outside Canva.
[ ] Verify picture, sound, spelling, duration, and final frame.
[ ] Use a clear filename and submit through your class workflow.

Lesson review is not producer approval of your actual video.
