Before you begin

Sign in to Canva. Have an interview clip and 2–3 B-roll clips ready. B-roll is footage that shows what the speaker describes. You can read the guide without footage.

The guided reading and checks take about 8½ minutes. Editing your own footage and upload/export waits take additional time. Canva is a separate product from Microsoft.

    Editor differences, sources & teacher notes

    This lesson uses Canva’s multi-track video timeline on desktop. Older projects and account rollouts can look different. If your project shows page thumbnails, create a new Video project to follow this guide; if offered, use the pencil icon to switch to Video Timeline. Menu names and available features can vary. Use Canva’s current help if your view differs.

    The campus volunteer example and name are fictional. The target of 30 seconds, simple cuts, readable titles, and a conservative 10% text inset are teaching choices, not Canva export requirements. Follow school consent, sourcing, branding, and delivery rules. Original or school-cleared footage is enough; no paid asset is required.

    Completion records review of the eight lesson steps and a correct knowledge check. It does not verify a video or submit student work. Progress is stored only in this browser if available. No accounts, uploads, or analytics are built into this lesson.
